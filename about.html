What is rolled ice cream?

Rolled ice cream began in Thailand and has become a favourite around the world for its smooth texture and fresh ingredients.

Rolled Ice Cream (Originally called I-Tim-Pad in Thailand, meaning Thai “Stir-Fried” Ice Cream) is a frozen dessert that started on the streets of Thailand around 2009.

Instead of being churned like traditional ice cream, a liquid ice cream base is poured onto an ultra-cold metal plate (around -20°C), where it freezes instantly. Fresh ingredients like fruit or chocolate are chopped and folded in, then the frozen mixture is scraped into tight rolls and served with toppings.

Because it freezes fast and isn’t churned like normal ice cream, it often ends up smoother and denser with more vibrant flavour. The rolling process also makes it a great interactive experience for events.
